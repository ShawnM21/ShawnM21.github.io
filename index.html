Solar photothermal propulsion

I lead lab operations for a solar photothermal propulsion program aimed at CubeSat maneuverability, covering prototype testing, CAD layout, mission modeling, and documentation. I fabricate the carbon nano-capillary foam specimens that drive light absorption and steam generation, then characterize them on a solar simulator: 3600-second runs logging mass loss against temperature and humidity, with linear regression on the weight trace to extract evaporation rate. Across four mix compositions the 20% cement foam reached 1.81 kg/m²/hr, and a reusability study on the same specimens found a one-time settling drop of about 14% after the first cycle followed by a stable plateau across three further cycles. I also machine the structural mounts, sensor fixtures, and thermal isolation hardware that keep chamber temperature data repeatable, and I contributed to the patent covering the propulsion test stand this work produced.

600 lbf N2O/IPA liquid engine

A pressure-fed liquid rocket engine running nitrous oxide and isopropanol at 600 psi chamber pressure, sized for 600 lbf of thrust over a 25 second burn. I set the design point at O/F 5.78 with a 6.65 expansion ratio, giving a 0.873 in throat and a 2.251 in exit on a conical contour, then sized a 16-on-16 ox-fuel-ox triplet injector to a 20% chamber pressure drop and fabricated the chamber. The feed budget closes at 832 psi supply, and an acoustic screening put the injection frequencies clear of the 1L, 1T, 2T, and 1R chamber modes.

LoRa recovery tracker

Custom PCBs built around an ESP32-S3, an RFM95W LoRa radio, and a u-blox SAM-M10Q GNSS module for live telemetry and recovery positioning. I did the board-level bring-up and debugging across RF, GNSS, sensor interfaces, and LiPo power, and wrote the firmware for the MPU6050 and BMP280/BMP390 sensors, Kalman-filtered apogee detection, adaptive LoRa rate control, and watchdog fault recovery. A 1S/2S power architecture with low-power startup modes gets about 10 hours out of a 400 mAh battery, and the board serves a live telemetry page over WiFi with raw NMEA output and over-the-air firmware upload for field updates.
